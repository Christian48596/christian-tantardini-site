About Christian

My research spans electronic-structure theory, chemical-bonding analysis, high-pressure materials and computational methods for complex physical systems. I connect fundamental questions — how atoms share charge, how solids respond and how structure controls properties — with predictive modelling.

I hold a PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology, an MSc in Chemistry from the University of Milan and a BSc in Chemistry and Industrial Chemistry from the University of Insubria.
