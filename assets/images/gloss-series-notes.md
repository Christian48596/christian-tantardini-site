# Glossy illustration series

Generated/edited with the built-in imagegen tool after the user requested shine instead of clay-like matte textures. The illustrations are conceptual decorative covers, not scientific data or official institutional artwork.

All alpha channels are retained. Website files are resized without cropping to 768 by 512 and encoded as WebP. Earlier research images and SVG activity illustrations are retained for rollback; raw PNG outputs remain in Codex generated_images.

## Shared final prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET. Restyle the same complete scientific/activity subject into a refined glossy scientific studio-render illustration. Preserve the recognizable subject, its general arrangement, proportions, palette families and complete silhouette. The user explicitly rejected the clay/plasticine look: remove ALL grain, rough matte texture, clay, ceramic, soft dough, toy-like rounded padding and chalkiness.
Materials: smooth precision-polished enamel with visible clean white specular reflections on atom spheres and colored solids; slender brushed/polished metal bonds and supports; clear lightly green-tinted optical glass for shell surfaces and electron-density volumes where applicable. Controlled gloss with crisp reflective highlights, like a professional scientific 3D visualization, not a plastic toy. Strong but tasteful light reflections, realistic surface smoothness, a soft upper-left rectangular studio softbox highlight and subtle opposite rim light. No neon, bloom, glitter, lens flare or reflective floor.
Keep forest-green/sage/olive as the dominant palette with small muted ochre, terracotta and dusty-blue accents. Deepen the contrast of forest green slightly if needed to make the gloss legible. Landscape 3:2, center the full object, leave 12 percent clear margins, true transparent alpha background, no black/white canvas or checkerboard. Maintain alpha transparency and use only a subtle contact shadow. No labels, readable text, logos, watermark, equations, people or extra decorative props.
