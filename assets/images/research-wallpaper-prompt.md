## Prompt

Create a wide landscape transparent-background illustration for an elegant scientist's personal website. This is a restrained editorial scientific wallpaper, not a poster or infographic. Five interconnected research themes: physical chemistry represented by fine electron-density contour lines around a small molecular geometry; solid-state physics represented by an accurate-looking geometric crystal lattice and unit cells; fluid dynamics represented by smooth streamlines passing around a few porous obstacles; machine learning represented by a sparse graph of connected nodes; artificial intelligence represented by an abstract optimization landscape with contour lines and a subtle path through it, no robot, brain or humanoid imagery. Integrate all five into one coherent flowing composition rather than five boxes. Composition: large clean completely transparent central area occupying at least 55 percent of the canvas, all scientific drawings confined to the left and right margins and lower outer corners, with delicate connecting lines near the edges. The center must have almost no marks because a name and portrait will be placed there. Panoramic 3:2 or wider landscape, ideally 1536 by 1024. Sophisticated precise pen-and-ink scientific engraving aesthetic with crisp fine lines, some lighter secondary construction lines, airy negative space, no rough sketch scribbles. Restrained forest green and muted olive green only, no blue, red, black-heavy fills, gradients, shadows, glows or 3D rendering. No labels, text, equations, lettering, logos or watermark. True transparent alpha background, do not depict a white paper, colored background or checkerboard. Lines should be moderately legible as an asset, the website will lower their opacity separately.
