Theme 01
Chemical bonding made measurable.

Topological analysis of the electron density can turn an abstract wavefunction into interpretable information about atoms, bonds and intermolecular interactions. This theme includes electronegativity, hydrogen bonding, van der Waals interactions and source-function analysis.

  • QTAIM
  • Electron density
  • Electronegativity
  • Non-covalent interactions

Theme 02
Materials beyond ambient intuition.

First-principles structure prediction reveals phases that emerge under pressure, reduced dimensionality or unusual composition. Representative studies include two-dimensional ionic materials, boride systems, hydrogen-rich solids and functionalized diamond-like films.

  • Crystal prediction
  • High pressure
  • 2D systems
  • Phonons

Theme 03
From quantum response to observable behaviour.

Electronic response, charge transport and spectroscopic observables require methods that remain accurate across heterogeneous systems. Current publications connect time-dependent density-functional theory, Kubo conductivity and scalable numerical representations.

  • TDDFT
  • Conductivity
  • Spectroscopy
  • Multiwavelets

Theme 04
Computation as scientific infrastructure.

Method development is part of the scientific question. The portfolio includes contributions to electronic-structure software, high-performance numerical workflows, constrained DFT and machine-learning models for material properties.

  • Scientific software
  • HPC
  • Machine learning
  • Method development
