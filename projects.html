Research

01 / 06

Bonding & quantum topology

 02 / 06

Materials under pressure & confinement

 03 / 06

Electronic-structure methods & software

Building accurate numerical methods and open scientific infrastructure.

 04 / 06

 06 / 06

Porous media & multiscale flow

Modelling multiphase transport and carbon-dioxide migration in geological media.
Theme 01
Chemical bonding and quantum topology

Christian's work uses electron-density topology, source functions and thermochemical cycles to turn many-electron calculations into chemically meaningful accounts of electronegativity, hydrogen bonding, van der Waals interactions and molecular crystals.

  • QTAIM
  • Electron density
  • Source function
  • Electronegativity
Key worksThermochemical electronegativities of the elementsVolumetric source function
Theme 02
Materials under pressure and confinement

Crystal-structure prediction and first-principles modelling reveal phases stabilized by pressure, reduced dimensionality or unconventional stoichiometry, including two-dimensional NaCl and silicene, W–Mo–B compounds and hydrogen-rich solids.

  • Crystal prediction
  • High pressure
  • 2D materials
  • Phonons
Key worksHexagonal two-dimensional NaCl crystalsSilicene under pressure
Theme 03
Electronic-structure methods and software

His method-development work spans constrained DFT, perturbation theory for metals, pseudopotential validation, multiresolution methods and community codes such as ABINIT and VAMPyR, with an emphasis on controlled accuracy and reproducibility.

  • DFPT
  • Constrained DFT
  • Multiwavelets
  • Open software
Key worksVariational DFPT for metalsABINIT: overview and focus on selected capabilities
Theme 04
Magnetism and data-driven materials

Physics-aware machine-learning potentials and interpretable descriptors address coupled spin–structure behaviour, material hardness and compositionally complex ceramics where conventional atomistic approximations become restrictive.

  • Magnetic MLIP
  • Symbolic regression
  • Alloys
  • High-entropy ceramics
Key worksMagnetic moment tensor potentials for Fe–AlInterpretable hardness descriptor
Theme 05
Spectroscopy and excited-state response

Response theory links electronic structure with measurable spectra and transport through electron–phonon response, conductivity, magnetic-resonance calculations and real-time or frequency-domain descriptions of excitation.

  • Spectroscopy
  • Bethe–Salpeter equation
  • TDDFT
  • Transport
Key worksElectron–phonon response in metalsReal-time multiwavelet response methods
Theme 06
Porous media and multiscale flow

At KFUPM, his modelling extends to multiphase transport in porous media, including Buckley–Leverett displacement, dynamic capillarity, representative elementary volumes and carbon-dioxide migration.

  • Porous media
  • Multiphase flow
  • CO₂ transport
  • Upscaling
