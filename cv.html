Profile

CV

Computational materials scientist with more than a decade of international experience in first-principles method development, high-performance computing and multiscale modelling for energy-relevant materials. His work spans quantum-chemical topology, electron–phonon interactions, many-body theory, charge transport, spectroscopy, high-throughput discovery and porous-media flow.
